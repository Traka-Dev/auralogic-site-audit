# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participate respectfully, give constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes and focus discussions on the work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sharing another person's private information, and persistent personal attacks are not acceptable. This applies to issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

Project maintainers may remove harmful content, request changes in behavior, or restrict participation. They will consider reports fairly and protect the privacy of those involved where possible.

Report concerns privately through [Aura Logic's contact channel](https://auralogic.dev/). Do not post sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers involved in a complaint should not be the sole decision maker on that complaint.
